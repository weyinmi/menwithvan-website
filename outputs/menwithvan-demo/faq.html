Help centre

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers for London house removals, office moves, Luton van hire, mileage, stairs, deposits and booking.

How is my quote calculated?

Your quote uses the number of Luton vans, movers, estimated hours, route mileage, pickup stairs, delivery stairs, congestion-zone status and VAT. The minimum booking is two hours.

How many movers can I book?

Each Luton van can carry up to three movers. One van allows up to three movers, two vans up to six movers, and five vans up to fifteen movers.

When is the congestion charge added?

The congestion charge is added only when the pickup or drop-off postcode/address is inside the London Congestion Charge zone.

Do lifts count as stairs?

No. If there is a working lift and the team can use it safely, there is no stair charge for that address.

Can I pay a deposit?

Yes. The booking flow supports a 25% deposit with the balance payable on completion, or full payment online through secure Stripe checkout once online payments are enabled.

Do you move offices?

Yes. Men With Van supports office removals, desks, chairs, boxes, monitors, light equipment and storage moves across London.
