Customer terms

Terms and conditions.

These terms explain how bookings, payments, cancellations, access, customer responsibilities and claims are handled for Men With Van removal services.

1. Who we are

These terms apply to removal, transport, packing and related services supplied by Men With Van (“we”, “us”, “our”) to the customer named on the booking (“you”, “your”). The confirmed quote, booking confirmation and these terms form the agreement between us.

2. Quotations and pricing

  1. Online quotations are guide prices until confirmed by us in writing.
  2. Quotes are calculated using Luton vans, movers, hours, mileage, stairs, congestion-zone status, VAT and any additional services requested.
  3. The minimum booking is two hours.
  4. We may amend a quote where the information supplied is incomplete or inaccurate, access is unsuitable, additional items are added, waiting time occurs, parking charges apply, stairs/lifts differ from the booking, or the job materially changes.
  5. VAT and statutory charges are payable in addition where applicable.

3. Booking and payment

  1. A booking is confirmed only when we have accepted it and any required deposit has cleared.
  2. Unless agreed otherwise, a 25% deposit is payable in advance and the balance is payable on completion.
  3. Where full payment is chosen online, payment must clear before the service starts.
  4. Unpaid balances may delay completion, release of goods or future services.

4. Cancellation and postponement

  1. If you cancel more than 48 hours before the move, we will normally refund the deposit unless specific non-refundable costs have already been incurred.
  2. If you cancel between 24 and 48 hours before the move, we may retain up to 50% of the deposit.
  3. If you cancel less than 24 hours before the move, we may retain the full deposit.
  4. If you request urgent or short-notice services, cancellation rights may be affected once work has started with your agreement.

5. Customer responsibilities

  1. You must provide accurate addresses, postcodes, contact details, inventory, access details, stair/lift information, parking restrictions and timing requirements.
  2. You or an authorised representative must be present at pickup and delivery unless agreed otherwise.
  3. You must ensure goods are properly packed unless we have agreed to pack them.
  4. You must arrange parking, building access, lift bookings, permits and concierge permissions where required.
  5. You must tell us about unusually heavy, fragile, valuable, hazardous or awkward items before the move.

6. Work not included unless agreed

Unless agreed in writing, we do not disconnect or reconnect appliances, remove fitted floor coverings, alter fixtures, move unsafe items, dismantle specialist furniture, handle hazardous goods, clear snow/ice/access routes, or perform work that our team reasonably believes is unsafe.

7. Excluded goods

We do not carry cash, jewellery, deeds, securities, dangerous goods, illegal items, animals, perishable goods, firearms, explosives, flammable substances, live plants unless agreed, or items that may cause infestation, contamination or damage. If such goods are supplied without disclosure, you are responsible for any loss, damage, cost, claim or disposal cost arising.

8. Delays and access

Arrival and completion times are estimates unless expressly confirmed in writing. We are not liable for delays caused by traffic, weather, building access, parking, customer readiness, inaccurate information, third parties or events outside our reasonable control. Waiting time and additional labour may be charged.

9. Loss, damage and insurance

  1. We will perform the service with reasonable care and skill.
  2. Our liability depends on the circumstances, declared value, packing method, insurance position and applicable law.
  3. Owner-packed goods are not covered for internal breakage unless there is clear external impact damage.
  4. We are not liable for ordinary wear, scratches, pre-existing defects, weak furniture, mechanical/electrical derangement without impact, or goods left inside drawers, wardrobes or appliances.
  5. Any insurance statements on the website or quote must match the actual live insurance policy wording.

10. Claims

You must inspect goods and premises as soon as reasonably possible. Any claim for loss, damage or property damage should be notified in writing with photographs and details within 24 hours of completion, or as soon as reasonably possible where this is not practical. Late notification may affect our ability to investigate and may affect insurance handling.

11. Right to withhold goods

Where lawful, we may withhold goods until all charges and costs due under the booking have been paid. Storage, redelivery and associated costs may be charged where goods are held due to non-payment or failed delivery.

12. Subcontracting

We may use trusted subcontractors or partner crews where appropriate. These terms continue to apply to the service.

13. Complaints and disputes

If something goes wrong, contact us promptly with your booking reference, photographs and a clear description of the issue. We will review complaints fairly and aim to resolve them without unnecessary delay.

14. Law

These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales. Nothing in these terms excludes liability where it would be unlawful to do so, including liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence.
